# fitbit-air-mac

A personal, single-user tool that shows the author's own Fitbit Air data (via the Google Health API) on the author's Mac:
live heart rate, daily totals, nightly vitals, sleep, and a morning coaching brief.

It is not distributed and has no users other than its author.

About

I'm a software engineer in Bengaluru. I started as a software engineer in Jaipur, moved into cloud support at AWS, where I helped companies move onto the cloud, then became a software development engineer at Amazon in 2019 and an SDE II in 2021.

Today I work on last-mile delivery for Amazon's quick-commerce business in India. Alongside that I build AI agents that engineers and operations teams use every day to investigate alarms and deliveries, and a developer tool that other teams have picked up for their own codebases.

I like closing the gap between an idea and something people can use. When a team is waiting on a dependency, I would rather build a first version in a couple of days and learn from it. That habit led to my conference talk and most of the tools on this page, and it helped two of my teams win hackathon awards.

I also teach. I run GenAI sessions for engineers, review designs and code, and mentor engineers.
